How it works

1 · One set a day

Everyone in the world gets the same puzzles each day, at the same level. They reset at midnight UTC.

2 · Guess, then learn

Answer first. Afterwards you get the explanation and the source, which is the part you actually remember.

3 · Keep a streak

Play on consecutive days to build a streak, and share a spoiler-free score grid when you are done.

About the content

Islamdle uses no images of prophets, companions or any person. Every puzzle is built from text, Arabic calligraphy and geometry — which is why there is no "guess the picture" mode here, and never will be.

Qur'anic text and surah details come from published references, and verse translations are M. Pickthall's public-domain rendering. Where scholars differ — on dating, on points of fiqh, on the order of events — the puzzle says so rather than presenting one view as the only one. Puzzles are written to be answerable without being an exam: if an average Muslim could not reasonably get there, it does not belong on Easy or Medium.

Found a mistake? That matters more here than anywhere else — tell us and it gets fixed.
