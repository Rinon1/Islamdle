Islamdle

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Islamdle is a daily puzzle game. There is no account, no sign-up and no login. This page explains exactly what is stored and what is not. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The short version

  • The game keeps your progress in your own browser. It is never uploaded.
  • There is nothing to sign up for. The only time an email is involved is if you donate and choose to use it to remove ads — and even then we keep only a scrambled fingerprint of it, never the address.
  • Analytics and advertising are off until you turn them on.
  • You can change your mind at any time from Privacy settings in the footer.

What is stored on your device

Islamdle uses your browser's local storage — not cookies — for the game itself. This data stays on your device, is readable only by this site, and is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • islamdle.v2 — today's puzzles, the guesses you have made, whether you solved each one, how long it took, and your streak.
  • islamdle.level — the difficulty you last picked.
  • islamdle.support.v1 — only if you open “Support Islamdle”: a random secret that produces your supporter code, and, once a donation is confirmed, the token that keeps ads off on this device.
  • islamdle.consent.v1 — the privacy choice you made here, and when you made it. This one is essential: without it you would be asked on every page load.

Clearing your browser's site data for Islamdle erases all of it, including your streak. We cannot restore it, because we never had a copy.

What is not collected

No name, phone number, postal address, payment details, account, contact list, location or device identifier is collected by this site, and no email address is stored (see “Supporting Islamdle” below). Nothing you type into the guess box is sent anywhere — guesses are checked entirely inside your browser against a data file the site downloads.

Analytics

If you allow analytics, Islamdle measures aggregate, anonymous usage — which modes are played, how many guesses a puzzle typically takes, and roughly where in the world traffic comes from — so the puzzles can be tuned. It is not used to identify you. If you decline, no analytics requests are made at all; the measurement scripts are never loaded.

Advertising

Islamdle is free and is funded by advertising. If you allow advertising cookies, our advertising partners may set cookies and similar identifiers to measure ads and to show ads relevant to you. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will be non-personalised — chosen from the page content rather than from anything about you.

Where Google advertising products are used, Islamdle signals your choice using Google Consent Mode, so advertising and measurement storage stay denied unless you have granted them.

Advertising partners in use will be named here, with links to their own privacy policies, before ads are switched on.

Supporting Islamdle

Donations are handled entirely by Buy Me a Coffee and its payment processor; we never see your card details. When you donate, Buy Me a Coffee tells Islamdle the donation's amount, currency, your message and the email you used. Islamdle keeps the amount and currency, any supporter code found in your message, and a one-way keyed hash of the email — enough to recognise the same email again, but not to read it back. That is all that is kept, and it is used only to decide whether ads should be removed.

If you use the “another device” option, the email you type is sent to Islamdle once, hashed the same way to look up your donation, and discarded. To have your supporter record deleted, contact us with the email or code you used.

Hosting

The site is served as static files. Like any web host, the hosting provider processes the technical information every web request necessarily contains — your IP address, the page requested, your browser's user-agent string and the time — in order to deliver the page and to protect against abuse. These are standard server logs and are not combined with anything else or used to build a profile. Islamdle is hosted on Netlify, which also stores the supporter records described above; its own privacy practices are described at netlify.com/privacy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data an organisation holds about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how it is used. Because Islamdle holds no account and stores your game data only on your own device, there is normally nothing for us to retrieve or delete — clearing your browser's site data does it immediately and completely. For anything your advertising or analytics choice may have involved, withdraw consent in Privacy settings, and contact us if you want more.

Children

Islamdle is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13 in particular. No personal data is knowingly collected from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the consent banner will be shown again so you can make a fresh choice. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions, corrections or requests: contact@[your-domain]. Corrections to the puzzle content are especially welcome.
